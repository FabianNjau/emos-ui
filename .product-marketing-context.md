# Product Marketing Context — EMOS

_Last updated: 2026-08-08_

---

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** EMOS is an evidence-based marketing intelligence platform that answers marketing questions with citations from real research.

**What it does:** EMOS ingests academic papers, textbooks, and industry research, then lets marketers ask questions in natural language and get answers backed by specific sources — not generic AI opinions.

**Product category:** Marketing intelligence / research tool / AI knowledge assistant

**Product type:** SaaS (web app)

**Business model:** Freemium
- Free: 10 chat sessions/month, 5 saved insights, 1 profile
- Pro: KES 1,500/month — unlimited sessions, insights, profiles, advanced AI
- Pro Max: KES 3,000/month — everything in Pro + advanced models, team collab, API access

---

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** Marketing professionals at startups and SMEs in East Africa (primarily Kenya), plus independent marketing consultants and small agency owners.

**Decision-makers:** Marketing managers, founders handling their own marketing, growth leads.

**Primary use case:** A marketer has a specific challenge — "How do I handle price objections?", "What's the best pricing strategy for a new SaaS?", "How do I reduce churn after the honeymoon period?" — and EMOS finds and synthesizes the relevant research.

**Jobs to be done:**
1. Get a fast, evidence-backed answer to a marketing question
2. Discover what academic research says about a specific tactic or strategy
3. Save key insights and sources for later reference
4. Build a personal knowledge base of marketing evidence

**Use cases:**
- Preparing a pricing strategy document
- Responding to a client brief with research-backed recommendations
- Learning what actually works in a specific marketing domain
- Building credibility with evidence-based proposals

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Most marketing advice is generic, anecdotal, or based on blog posts. Marketers making real decisions have no reliable way to distinguish evidence from opinion.

**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Google search returns blog posts, opinions, and ads — not research
- ChatGPT hallucinate specifics and cite non-existent papers
- Traditional research databases (JSTOR, etc.) are inaccessible without academic credentials
- Consultants charge high fees for research that's available in open-access papers

**What it costs them:** Bad strategic decisions based on weak evidence, client pitches that lack credibility, strategies that underperform because they relied on the wrong framework.

**Emotional tension:** The fear of presenting advice that turns out to be wrong. The frustration of spending hours researching only to find conflicting or low-quality information.

---

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Perplexity (general AI search) — falls short because it doesn't specialize in marketing research, hallucinates academic citations, and has no concept of a personal knowledge workspace.

**Secondary:** Traditional search (Google/Bing) — falls short because it returns blog posts instead of research, requires heavy filtering, and has no memory of what you've already explored.

**Indirect:** Hiring a marketing consultant or research analyst — falls short because it's slow (days vs. seconds), expensive (KES 50k+ vs. KES 1,500/mo), and not accessible to small teams.

---

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**
- Marketing-domain-specific research corpus (not general web search)
- Answers cite specific sources — verifiable, not hallucinated
- Personal knowledge workspace: save, organize, and revisit insights
- Built for East African market (KES pricing, relevant examples)

**How we do it differently:** EMOS is a research tool purpose-built for marketing decisions. It retrieves from a curated corpus and generates cited answers — not a general AI with a marketing veneer.

**Why that's better:** Answers are traceable to real sources. Users can click through to the original paper. Knowledge accumulates over time.

**Why customers choose us:** Speed (answer in seconds vs. hours of searching) + credibility (cited research vs. AI opinions) + personal knowledge building (save and revisit insights)

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "How is this different from ChatGPT?" | ChatGPT generalizes and sometimes invents citations. EMOS retrieves from a specific marketing research corpus and always cites its sources. |
| "I can just Google this" | Google returns blog posts and opinions. EMOS returns research-backed answers with specific citations you can verify. |
| "Is the research actually relevant to my market?" | The corpus covers foundational marketing science — psychology, pricing, consumer behavior — that applies across markets. East Africa-specific cases are being added. |
| "Is my data private?" | Your sessions and saved insights are private to your account. The research corpus is shared. |

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Hours wasted searching for evidence, generic AI answers that don't cite sources, no way to save and revisit findings.

**Pull:** Answers in seconds with verifiable citations, personal knowledge workspace that grows over time, evidence-backed recommendations that win client confianza.

**Habit:** Asking colleagues, searching Google, reading blog posts, accepting generic AI answers.

**Anxiety:** Will the research corpus be large enough? Will citations be accurate? Is this just another AI hallucination tool?
